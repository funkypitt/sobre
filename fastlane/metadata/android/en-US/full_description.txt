Sobre is a text-only, audio-only player for your YouTube subscriptions.

It plays only the audio of the videos published by channels you add yourself. Zero images: no thumbnails, no avatars, no icons. Zero video: the app never reads a video stream. Zero algorithmic feed: no recommendations, no trending, no Shorts, no comments, no infinite scroll. The only source of content is the list of channels you explicitly subscribed to.

- Three tabs: Feed, Channels, Downloads
- Add a channel by pasting its URL, or share the URL to Sobre
- Import and export subscriptions as OPML
- Background playback with lock-screen and Bluetooth controls
- Download episodes for offline listening
- Short videos (under 5 minutes) are hidden from feeds
- High-contrast text interface, suitable for e-ink screens

Feeds come from YouTube's own Atom RSS; audio streams are resolved with NewPipeExtractor. No account, no ads, no tracking.
